About the Foundation

Our Story

Omkar Health and Education Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit company established with the sole purpose of serving disadvantaged, economically weaker and underserved communities across India.

We believe that access to quality healthcare and education is a fundamental right, not a privilege. Through our programs, we strive to bridge the gap and ensure that every individual — regardless of their economic background — has the opportunity to lead a healthy, educated and productive life.

As a non-profit organisation, every rupee received goes directly towards our programs and beneficiaries. We do not distribute profits or dividends to our members.

Non-Profit Character

The Foundation shall apply its income and profits, if any, solely towards the promotion of its charitable objects.

No portion shall be paid or transferred, directly or indirectly, by way of dividend, bonus or otherwise by way of profit to its members — in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013.

What We Stand For

Our Mission & Objectives

Our foundation is guided by the following objectives as set out in our Memorandum of Association.

🏥01

Healthcare Infrastructure

To promote, protect and improve health and well-being of the public, particularly disadvantaged, economically weaker and underserved communities, by establishing hospitals, clinics, dispensaries, diagnostic centres, mobile medical units, health camps and telemedicine facilities.

💊02

Affordable Healthcare

To provide, promote and facilitate affordable and accessible healthcare services including preventive, promotive, curative and rehabilitative healthcare, maternal and child healthcare, nutrition, sanitation, mental well-being and public health awareness.

🎓03

Education & Literacy

To promote education and literacy by establishing, supporting and managing schools, educational institutions, learning centres, vocational training centres, skill-development centres, libraries and digital learning facilities for children, youth, women and underserved sections of society.

📚04

Educational Assistance

To provide educational assistance including scholarships, fellowships, study materials, books, digital resources, vocational training, career guidance, coaching, mentoring and other support to deserving and economically disadvantaged persons.

🔧05

Skill Development

To undertake programmes for skill development, vocational education, employability and livelihood enhancement, particularly for youth, women and persons from disadvantaged communities.

🔬06

Research & Awareness

To conduct and support research, studies, surveys and awareness programmes in healthcare, education, nutrition, sanitation, public health and skill development, and to disseminate knowledge and best practices for public benefit.

🤝07

Community Development

To undertake community development and social welfare activities including awareness campaigns, outreach programmes, camps, workshops, seminars and training programmes for the benefit of the public.

🏛️08

Collaboration

To collaborate with government authorities, local bodies, educational institutions, hospitals, healthcare professionals, charitable organisations, corporates, foundations and international organisations for carrying out the objects of the Company.

💻09

Technology & Digital

To promote the use of technology and digital solutions in health and education, including e-learning, digital literacy, telemedicine, online educational resources and digital health awareness for improving access to services.

⚖️010

Charitable Activities

To undertake any other charitable activities incidental or conducive to the attainment of the above objects, in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013 and other applicable laws.
